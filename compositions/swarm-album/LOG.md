### Observations

1. **Sparse vs Dense:** The coupling parameter has dramatic effect. Low coupling (0.03-0.05) creates meditative sparse textures. High coupling (0.20-0.25) creates dense, active passages.

2. **Scale Choice:** Japanese scale (In-sen) works beautifully for meditative sections. Blues scale creates interesting tension in the complex middle section.

3. **Pattern Effects:** 
   - Spiral pattern creates most chaotic/interesting dynamics
   - Islands pattern creates call-response between regions
   - Barrier creates splitting/rejoining waves

4. **Pacemaker Count:** Single pacemaker = simple pulse. Multiple pacemakers = polyrhythmic complexity.
